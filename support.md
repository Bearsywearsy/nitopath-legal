## What NitoPath is

NitoPath is a navigation app with turn-by-turn directions that also shows where automated license plate readers and other traffic cameras are mapped. Seeing every camera and each route's camera count is free. NitoPath Pro plans routes that pass fewer cameras (or more, if you prefer monitored roads) and warns you before each one. It's on the App Store for iPhone, coming to Google Play, and [in your browser](/app/).

## Subscriptions

<details id="free-vs-pro"><summary>What's free and what's Pro?</summary>
<p><strong>Free:</strong> navigation with voice, search, stops, saved places, trip history, the camera map, camera details and each route's camera count.</p>
<p><strong>Pro:</strong> camera-aware routing (fewer or more cameras), spoken and vibrating camera alerts, Drive mode and custom 3D location pins. Pro is $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year, both with a 3-day free trial. See <a href="/pricing/">Pricing</a>.</p>
</details>

<details id="pro-not-unlocking"><summary>I paid but Pro didn't unlock</summary>
<p>Your subscription is tied to your NitoPath account, and the app checks it with our server. Most of the time it unlocks within a minute. If it hasn't:</p>
<ol>
<li>Make sure you're signed in to the same NitoPath account you subscribed with (menu → Account).</li>
<li>Close the app completely (swipe it away) and open it again.</li>
<li>Still locked? Open NitoPath Pro and tap <strong>Restore purchase</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>If none of that works, email us from your account's address with the date you subscribed. We'll sort it out, and you won't be charged twice.</p>
</details>

<details id="restore"><summary>How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?</summary>
<p>Sign in with the same NitoPath account, then open NitoPath Pro from the menu or Settings → Subscription and tap <strong>Restore purchase</strong>. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with. Signing in to the web app with that account gives you Pro there too.</p>
</details>

<details id="cancel"><summary>How do I cancel?</summary>
<p>Subscriptions are handled by the store you paid through, so cancel there:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>iPhone:</strong> Settings → your name → Subscriptions → NitoPath → Cancel Subscription.</li>
<li><strong>Android:</strong> Google Play → your profile picture → Payments &amp; subscriptions → Subscriptions → NitoPath → Cancel.</li>
</ul>
<p>Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. If you cancel during the 3-day trial, you aren't charged. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel a subscription.</p>
</details>

<details id="refund"><summary>Can I get a refund?</summary>
<p>Apple and Google handle refunds under their own policies. Apple: <a href="https://reportaproblem.apple.com">reportaproblem.apple.com</a>. Google: open your order history in Google Play and request one there.</p>
</details>

## Cameras

<details id="report-camera"><summary>A camera is missing or in the wrong place</summary>
<p>Long-press the spot on the map and tap <strong>Report camera</strong>, or tap the camera button while navigating to report one you just passed. You need to be signed in, which keeps spam off the map. We review every report. You can also add it to OpenStreetMap through <a href="https://deflock.org/report">deflock.org/report</a>, which is where most of our camera data comes from.</p>
</details>

<details id="no-cameras"><summary>The map is empty or shows no cameras</summary>
<p>The camera map is free for everyone. Check that cameras are switched on in Settings → Display, and that you're online, then close and reopen the app.</p>
</details>

## The app

<details id="routes"><summary>Routes are slow or fail</summary>
<p>Long trips are planned in sections, which can take a few seconds. If a route fails, check your connection and try again. If it keeps happening, send us the start and end points.</p>
</details>

<details id="voice"><summary>Voice guidance is silent</summary>
<p>Make sure your phone isn't on silent, turn up media volume while navigating, and check that voice is on in Settings → Alerts &amp; voice (there's a volume slider and a Test voice button).</p>
</details>

<details id="password"><summary>I forgot my password</summary>
<p>On the sign-in screen tap <strong>Forgot password</strong>. The email link opens a page on nitopath.com where you choose a new one. Check spam if it doesn't arrive in a few minutes.</p>
</details>

## Your account

<details id="delete-account"><summary>How do I delete my account?</summary>
<p>In the app, open the menu → Account → <strong>Delete account</strong>. That removes your account, saved places, history and settings from our servers right away. Can't get into the app? See <a href="/delete-account/">Delete your account</a>. Remember to cancel any subscription in the App Store or Google Play first.</p>
</details>

<details id="privacy"><summary>What do you do with my data?</summary>
<p>As little as we can. Camera alerts are worked out on your phone, your location trail is never uploaded, and we don't sell your data or show ads. The <a href="/privacy-policy.html">Privacy Policy</a> has the details.</p>
</details>
