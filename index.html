    What are license plate readers?

    Cameras that read every passing plate and log where and when it was seen. Learn how they work, who runs them and how to find your local policy.

    Where the data comes from

    Camera locations come from OpenStreetMap, mapped by volunteers, many through the DeFlock project. NitoPath refreshes its copy every few hours. Crowdsourced data is never complete, so a route with no known cameras can still pass ones nobody has mapped yet.
