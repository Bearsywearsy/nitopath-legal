NitoPath now lives at nitopath.com. This is where you'll find release news, help, and our policies.

NitoPath is a driving navigation app that shows where automated license plate readers are and plans routes that pass fewer of them, or more of them. Navigation is free; camera features are part of NitoPath Pro.

We're getting ready to launch on the App Store and Google Play. Check back here for the release date.
