## In the app

1. Open NitoPath and sign in.
2. Go to **Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and can't be undone.

## Without the app

Email [Nitopath@gmail.com](mailto:Nitopath@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20account) **from the email address on the account** with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm once it's done.

## Delete some of your data and keep your account

In the app, open **Saved & history** (from the menu on the map, or Account → Saved places, routes & history):

- **Saved places and saved routes:** tap the bin icon next to any one to delete it.
- **Trip history:** tap **Clear history** to delete every trip.

Recent searches are kept only on your phone. Clear them under **Settings → Clear recent searches**.

These deletions happen immediately on our servers and can't be undone. To have anything else removed, such as a camera report, email [Nitopath@gmail.com](mailto:Nitopath@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20some%20of%20my%20data) from the address on the account and say what to remove.

## What gets deleted

Your profile, settings, saved places, saved routes, trip history and camera reports. Backups may keep copies for a short period before they're overwritten. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy.html).

## Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription, because billing goes through Apple or Google. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first. See [How do I cancel?](/faq/#subscription)
