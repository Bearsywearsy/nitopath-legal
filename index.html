NitoPath

Driving directions that show where automated license plate readers are, so you can plan routes that pass fewer of them, or more of them.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

How it works

Some drivers want to pass fewer cameras. Others would rather stay on monitored roads. NitoPath does both, and is honest about what it knows.

See the cameras

Every license plate reader that volunteers have mapped in OpenStreetMap, shown on the map around you.

Choose your route

Compare routes by travel time and camera count, then pick the one that suits the drive.

Drive with alerts

Turn-by-turn guidance with spoken directions and a heads-up as you approach a camera.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, settings, saved places, saved routes, trip history and camera reports. If you cannot open the app, email Nitopath@gmail.com from the address on the account.

Camera data is crowdsourced and may be incomplete or out of date. Always obey traffic laws and posted signs.

Camera locations © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), largely mapped by the DeFlock community. Routing and search by openrouteservice, with OSRM and Photon as fallbacks.
