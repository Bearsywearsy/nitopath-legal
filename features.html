Features

A complete navigation app, with license plate reader data built in.

Camera features NitoPath Pro

{% include icons/camera.svg %}

Cameras on the map

Every mapped automated license plate reader around you, grouped into clusters when you zoom out.

{% include icons/route.svg %}

Fewer cameras, or more

Plan a route that passes fewer cameras, or one that stays on monitored roads. The fastest sensible route stays the default, so you never get a huge detour by surprise.

{% include icons/clock.svg %}

Compare side by side

See each route's time, distance and camera count before you pick one.

{% include icons/bell.svg %}

Camera alerts

Spoken and vibrating warnings before each camera on your route, calculated on your phone.

{% include icons/nav.svg %}

Drive mode

Camera warnings while you drive with no destination set, with your speed on screen.

{% include icons/edit.svg %}

Upcoming turns and cameras

A list of every turn ahead with cameras slotted in at the right point.

Navigation Free

{% include icons/nav.svg %}

Turn-by-turn

Spoken directions, automatic rerouting, and a full-screen view with speed, arrival time and distance left.

{% include icons/pin.svg %}

Search and stops

Address search with suggestions as you type, up to 8 stops, and avoid highways or tolls.

{% include icons/bookmark.svg %}

Saved places and trips

Save home, work and favourites, keep your trip history, and sync it between devices with an account.

{% include icons/camera.svg %}

Report a camera

Spot one that isn't on the map? Send it in from the app and we'll review it.

{% include icons/shield.svg %}

Private by design

No account needed, no background tracking, no selling or advertising with your data.

{% include icons/route.svg %}

Your kind of map

Dark mode, satellite and terrain maps, and live traffic.

Know what it can't do

NitoPath is an informational aid. It doesn't make you anonymous, it can't stop you being recorded, and no route is guaranteed camera-free. Always obey traffic laws and posted signs.
