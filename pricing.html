How billing works

You subscribe inside the app, and payment goes through your Apple ID or Google Play account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. If you cancel during the free trial, you aren't charged.

To cancel, open your subscriptions in the App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions). Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

Questions about a charge? See the FAQ or contact support.
