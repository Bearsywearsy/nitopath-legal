2. What NitoPath is — and is not

NitoPath is an informational navigation aid. It shows the locations of automated licence plate readers (ALPRs) that volunteers have recorded in OpenStreetMap, and it can plan driving routes that pass fewer of them or that pass more of them, whichever you choose. People use it in both directions: some prefer fewer cameras, others prefer to travel on monitored roads.

Camera data is crowdsourced and is always incomplete, and it may be wrong, outdated, or missing entirely.
