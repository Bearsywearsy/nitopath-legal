How many license plate cameras are in {{ s.name }}?

Can I plan a drive that passes fewer of them, or more?

Yes. Every NitoPath route shows how many mapped cameras it passes, free. NitoPath Pro ($3.99 a month or $29.99 a year) plans the route with the fewest cameras or the most.

A camera is missing. How do I add it?

Report it from the NitoPath app, or add it directly at deflock.org/report. Once it is in OpenStreetMap, it is counted here within a day.

About these numbers

Camera locations come from OpenStreetMap, where volunteers (many through the DeFlock project) map automated license plate readers by hand. A state with a low count may simply have fewer volunteers, so treat these as the cameras known so far, not every camera that exists. You can add a missing one from the NitoPath app.
