## Quick fixes

### The map is empty or there are no cameras
The camera map is free for everyone. If you see no cameras, check that the cameras switch is on in Settings under Display, and that you have a connection, then close and reopen the app.

### Routes take a long time or fail
Very long trips are planned in sections, which can take a few seconds. If it fails, check your connection and try again. Tell us the start and end points if it keeps happening.

### Voice guidance is silent
Check your phone isn't on silent, turn up media volume while navigating, and make sure voice is switched on in the app's Settings.

### I forgot my password
On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot password**. The email link opens a page on nitopath.com where you choose a new one.

### I want to cancel my subscription
See [How do I cancel?](/faq/#subscription) in the FAQ.

## Support the project

NitoPath is independent. If it's useful to you and you'd like to chip in, you can buy us a coffee. It's entirely optional and doesn't change anything in the app.
