## The app

<details><summary>What is an ALPR?</summary>
<p>An automated license plate reader is a camera that photographs passing vehicles and reads their plates automatically, usually recording the time and place. They're mounted on poles, traffic lights and police cars. Our <a href="/alpr/">explainer</a> has more.</p>
</details>

<details><summary>Is NitoPath for avoiding cameras?</summary>
<p>It's for knowing where they are. Some people prefer routes with fewer cameras; others prefer to stay on monitored roads. NitoPath plans either way and leaves the choice to you.</p>
</details>

<details><summary>Which phones does it run on?</summary>
<p>NitoPath is coming to iPhone and Android. Follow the <a href="/news/">News</a> page for release dates.</p>
</details>

<details><summary>Can I use NitoPath on a computer?</summary>
<p>Yes. <a href="/app/">Open it in your browser</a> to search, plan and compare routes, and export them as GPX. Subscriptions are bought in the phone app; sign in on the web with the same account and the camera features are there too.</p>
</details>

<details><summary>Do I need an account?</summary>
<p>No. Navigation, search and saved places work without one. An account lets your saved places, settings and trip history follow you between devices, and lets you report cameras.</p>
</details>

## Camera data

<details><summary>Where does the camera data come from?</summary>
<p>From OpenStreetMap, where volunteers map cameras, many of them through the DeFlock project. NitoPath refreshes its copy every few hours.</p>
</details>

<details><summary>Is it complete?</summary>
<p>No, and it never will be. A route with no known cameras can still pass cameras nobody has mapped, recently installed ones, or mobile ones. NitoPath doesn't make you anonymous and doesn't make any route safe.</p>
</details>

<details><summary>A camera is missing or in the wrong place. What do I do?</summary>
<p>Use <strong>Report a camera</strong> in the app (you'll need to be signed in). We review reports and approved ones can be added to the map. You can also add it directly at <a href="https://deflock.org/report">deflock.org/report</a>.</p>
</details>

## Subscription

<details><summary>What's free and what's Pro?</summary>
<p>Navigation, search, multiple stops, saved places and trip history are free. Cameras on the map, camera-aware routing, camera alerts and Drive mode are Pro. See <a href="/pricing/">Pricing</a>.</p>
</details>

<details><summary>How do I cancel?</summary>
<p>Through the store you subscribed with: the App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Profile → Payments and subscriptions). Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.</p>
</details>

<details><summary>Can I get a refund?</summary>
<p>Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. Apple: <a href="https://reportaproblem.apple.com">reportaproblem.apple.com</a>. Google: request it from your order history in Google Play.</p>
</details>

## Privacy

<details><summary>Does NitoPath track my location?</summary>
<p>Only while the app is open and you've allowed it. Camera alerts are worked out on your phone. Your continuous location trail is never uploaded. Full details are in the <a href="/privacy-policy.html">Privacy Policy</a>.</p>
</details>

<details><summary>Do you sell my data?</summary>
<p>No. We don't sell personal information, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising.</p>
</details>

<details><summary>How do I delete my account?</summary>
<p>In the app, open Account → Delete account. More options on the <a href="/delete-account/">Delete your account</a> page.</p>
</details>
